💡 About Highlighted Settings

Settings shown in amber are critical hardware parameters specific to the Schabmuller TSA240-200-210 motor. These include:

  • Saturation Frequency (50 Hz): Motor magnetic saturation point
  • Braking Modulation (50 Hz): Regenerative braking frequency
  • Boost Settings: Voltage boost at low and high frequencies for proper torque
  • Slip Control: Parameters for traction control and wheel slip prevention
