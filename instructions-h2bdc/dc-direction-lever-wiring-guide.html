🔌 DC Installation Instructions

Important: This switch controls the direction of the FLEXI DC (ZAPI CONTROLLER) ELFIN system. Wire connections include: Wire No. 10 (top on both sides), Wire No. 41 (side connections on both views), Wire No. 7 (bottom left), Wire No. 13 (bottom center - front of truck direction), Wire No. 14 (bottom right view), and Wire No. 42 (bottom center right view).

Safety Note: Always disconnect power before servicing. This is a DC controller system - ensure proper polarity and verify all wire connections before operation. The lever should point toward the front of the truck for proper orientation. Improper wiring can result in incorrect directional control or equipment damage.
