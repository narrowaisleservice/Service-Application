⚡ Installation Notes
🔌
Connection Overview This switch controls the direction of the FLEXI AC/ACION ELFIN system. Wire No. 6 (RED/BLACK) provides power to the switch and appears on both sides. Wire No. 13 (YELLOW/BLACK) and Wire No. 14 (BLUE/BLACK) control the forward and reverse functions respectively. Ensure all connections are secure and properly insulated before operation. The lever should point toward the front of the truck for proper orientation.
⚠️
Safety Note Always disconnect power before servicing. Verify all wire connections match the color coding shown. Improper wiring can result in incorrect directional control or equipment damage.
