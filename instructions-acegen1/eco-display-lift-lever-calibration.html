✓

All four voltage points programmed. Cycle the key to finalise. Verify lift/lower operation before returning to service.

Critical Reminders:
  • Always keep the lever in position when pressing Out (Key 2) to lock values
  • Return to rest position after each calibration point
  • Voltage values are approximate — slight variations are normal
  • Four calibration points: Min Lift (~1.2V), Full Lift (~1.9V), Full Lower (~0.2V), Min Lower (~0.9V)
  • Must save changes at the end or calibration will be lost
